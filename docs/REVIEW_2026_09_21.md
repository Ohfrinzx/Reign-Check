## Review findings fixed

| Finding | Effect before the fix | Fix and verification |
|---|---|---|
| Consequence IDs depended on a module-global counter (`effects.ts`) | Repeating a saved decision after another run or React updater replay could yield different state. | Saved `flags.__effectId`; exact full-state replay tests, including interleaved runs and JSON reloads. |
| Paid promises remained unresolved (`cards.ts`, `followups.ts`, `effects.ts`) | A paid promise increased the kept count, then later lapsed and penalized the player. Reneging could count twice. | Explicit named resolution and negotiated extensions; tests for all three promise families, lapse immunity and one-time counting. |
| Construction upkeep was charged directly and through the budget (`engine.ts`) | Projects cost twice their stated daily upkeep until their completion day. | Budget pays active projects once; the completing project's final day is charged once too. Tests cover 1/2/3 days remaining. |
| Decisions could resolve more than once (`engine.ts`) | Repeat activation could duplicate costs and rewards. | Phase guards on decisions and transitions; duplicate-action regression and input-state immutability test. |
| Empty decks skipped `finishDay()` (`engine.ts`) | No summary or confidence/ending check on a day with no eligible cards. | Empty days use the normal end-of-day path; regression verifies ending and summary. |
| Negative treasury blocked incoming money (`shop.ts`, `engine.ts`) | Cash-paying deals and zero-cash exits could be rejected as unaffordable. | Only positive cash costs require a sufficient balance; tests cover payouts and free exits in debt. |
| Favour spending replaced an active decision receipt (`engine.ts`) | A caller spending a favour during resolution could lose the decision outcome. Ended states could also change. | Preserve active receipts; block mutations after an ending; regression tests. |
| Honorific IDs leaked into dialogue (`state.ts`, `Intro.tsx`) | Selecting Ma'am stored `maam`; Chair stored lowercase `chair`. | Store the spoken word and resolve either ID or word in the intro; unit and browser checks. |
| Global keyboard shortcuts intercepted focused buttons (`App.tsx`) | Enter on Menu/Buy could advance the phase instead of activating the button. | Native focused controls own Enter/Space; real-browser Menu and Buy tests. |
| Continue trusted stale metadata (`App.tsx`) | The title offered an old run that the version-guarded loader could not open. | Detect a readable saved game; real-browser version-6 rejection and version-7 resume. |
| Fullscreen shop exit was below long offers (`Shop.tsx`) | Leaving an act shop required scrolling despite the documented reachability rule. | Put the single Leave action above offers; browser bounding-box assertion at 1366×700. |
| Content integrity omitted `CARDS2` | The later standard-card pool bypassed ID/reference/character checks. | Include every card registry, including new mandate content. |
| Browser scripts used author-machine paths and did not always fail on errors | Checks could be unusable elsewhere or appear successful despite an incomplete run. | Installed Chromium or explicit executable override, portable screenshots, assertions, one-process Vite runner; update stale alert selectors. |

The review covered the engine/state/effects, economy and endings, Back Room,
save/loading, displayed resources and factions, UI actions/screens, content
registries and follow-up wiring, and verification tooling. It is not a claim
that testing proves the absence of all bugs or that every content choice has
been balanced.

## Verification

- Baseline: 60 tests passed and production build succeeded before changes.
- Final unit/simulation suite: 85 tests pass, including the existing 200-run
  simulation and 25 new mandate/regression tests.
- Every mandate is tested through an ending with full-state comparisons on
  every step against a JSON-save/reload replay.
- Production build: TypeScript and Vite pass.
- Chromium at 1366×700: six origins, visible rules, start-button reachability,
  reload/resume, incompatible-save rejection, focused keyboard buttons,
  Handover price/actual debit, full act shop exit, act transition, full run,
  aggressive ending/restart, and a multi-day save/reload playthrough.
- Original browser checks remain runnable individually; `npm run test:browser`
  starts its own Vite server and runs the mandate check plus all three.
- Shop probe: 120 runs × 4 buying strategies × 2 choice policies before and
  after. Random-choice buyers averaged 12.8–13.7 days before and 13.4–14.2
  after; reached-day-18 rates were 22–36% before and 28–43% after. First-option
  play still reaches day 18 in 99–100% of these post-change probe groups.
  These compare the entire change set, including different origins and bug
  fixes; they do not isolate a mandate's causal effect or establish balance.
