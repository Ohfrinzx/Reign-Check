Payroll
Is Short

$11 billion missing, eleven days before the state has to pay one in six working adults.
KEL BRASK · Finance Minister · has read the whole budget

Brask has the green notebook open, which means these are the real numbers.

“State payroll clears on the 28th. That is one in six working adults in this country. At the current rate we are $11 billion short and I have run out of ways to describe it as a timing difference.”

He is not sweating. That is how you know he is not exaggerating.

The shortfall has been carried forward twice. Neither the banks nor the central bank will move without being asked directly, and both will want something for it.
